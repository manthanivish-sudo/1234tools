About time units

Time is measured in seconds, the SI base unit, defined by a fixed number of oscillations of the caesium-133 atom: 9,192,631,770 of them make one second. Minutes, hours and days are not powers of ten: 60 seconds make a minute, 60 minutes an hour and 24 hours a day, so a day is 86,400 seconds and a week is 604,800. Months and years vary in length, so a converter has to use an average. The year here is the Julian year of 365.25 days, the average over a four-year leap cycle and the year astronomers use; the month is one twelfth of it, 30.4375 days. A millisecond is a thousandth of a second, the unit of computer timings and reaction times. For calendar sums with real dates, leap years and time zones, a date calculator is the better tool.

Frequently asked questions

How many seconds are in a day?

86,400 seconds: 24 hours of 60 minutes of 60 seconds.

How many minutes are in a day?

1,440 minutes, and 10,080 in a week.

How many hours are in a week?

168 hours: 7 days of 24 hours.

How many days are in a year?

A calendar year has 365 days, or 366 in a leap year. This converter uses the Julian year, the average over a four-year leap cycle: 1 yr = 365.25 d.

How many weeks are in a year?

52.1786 wk in a Julian year of 365.25 days. A 365-day calendar year is 52 weeks and 1 day.
