About speed units

Speed is distance travelled per unit of time. The SI unit is the metre per second (m/s). Road speeds are given in kilometres per hour in most countries and in miles per hour in the UK and the US. A metre per second is exactly 3.6 km/h, and a mile per hour is exactly 1.609344 km/h because the mile is defined as 1,609.344 m. Ships and aircraft use the knot, one nautical mile per hour, which is exactly 1.852 km/h or about 1.151 mph. Feet per second appear in ballistics and engineering. Mach gives a speed as a multiple of the speed of sound, which depends on the temperature of the air; this converter uses 340.29 m/s, the standard value at sea level, so treat Mach figures for high altitude as approximate.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert km/h to mph?

Divide by 1.609344, the number of kilometres in a mile. 100 km/h is 62.1371 mph, and 50 km/h is 31.0686 mph.

How do I convert mph to km/h?

Multiply by 1.609344. 30 mph is 48.2803 km/h, and 70 mph is 112.6541 km/h.

How do I convert m/s to km/h?

Multiply by 3.6: an hour is 3,600 seconds and a kilometre is 1,000 metres. 10 m/s is 36 km/h.

How fast is a knot?

A knot is one nautical mile per hour: exactly 1.852 km/h, or 1.1508 mph.

What is Mach 1 in km/h?

This converter uses the speed of sound at sea level in standard conditions, 340.29 m/s, so Mach 1 is 1,225.04 km/h (761.2071 mph). Sound travels more slowly in colder air, so Mach 1 is slower at cruising altitude.
