Convert Millibar to PSI

Convert Millibar (mbar) to PSI (psi) instantly. Works in both directions, with a full pressure table.

How to convert millibars to psi

psi ≈ mbar × 0.01450377377

To convert millibars to psi, multiply the pressure by about 0.01450377377. The factor is shown to 10 significant figures; the converter above uses the full value. In the other direction, 1 psi = 68.9476 mbar.

Worked example100 mbar × 0.01450377377 ≈ 1.4504 psi

A millibar is the same as a hectopascal (hPa).
