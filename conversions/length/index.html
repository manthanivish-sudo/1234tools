About length units

Length is the distance between two points. The SI unit is the metre (m), defined since 1983 by the distance light travels in a vacuum in 1/299,792,458 of a second. Metric units step by powers of ten: a kilometre is 1,000 m, and a metre is 100 cm or 1,000 mm. The imperial and US customary units were redefined against the metre in 1959: the international inch is exactly 2.54 cm, so a foot (12 in) is exactly 0.3048 m, a yard (3 ft) is 0.9144 m and a mile (1,760 yd) is 1,609.344 m. The UK still signs roads in miles and often gives heights in feet and inches; most of the world uses metric units for everything. The nautical mile, exactly 1,852 m, is used at sea and in the air, and the astronomical unit, light year and parsec measure distances in space.

Frequently asked questions

How many centimetres are in an inch?

One inch is exactly 2.54 cm. The international inch has been defined as 2.54 cm since 1959, so this conversion is exact.

How many feet are in a metre?

One metre is 3.2808 ft, or 3 ft 3.3701 in. Going the other way, a foot is exactly 0.3048 m.

How do I convert miles to kilometres?

Multiply by 1.609344, the exact number of kilometres in a mile. 10 miles is 16.0934 km, and 100 km is 62.1371 mi.

What is 5 feet 10 inches in centimetres?

177.8 cm: 5 ft is 152.4 cm and 10 in is 25.4 cm. For any height, multiply the feet by 30.48 and the inches by 2.54, then add them.

How many millimetres are in an inch?

Exactly 25.4 mm. A millimetre is 0.03937 in.
