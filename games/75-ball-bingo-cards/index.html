75-Ball Bingo Cards to Print

Classic B-I-N-G-O cards with a free centre, four to a page, each with its own number — and a caller that draws 1–75 on the big screen.

  • No sign-up

Why people use it

For families and clubs playing classic 75-ball bingo

Bought bingo pads run out, and a call from the bag is slow without a board in front of everyone.

The usual way

  • ✗Pads of cards that run out
  • ✗A call-out from a paper list
  • ✗Cards with a duplicate in the column

With this tool

✓B-I-N-G-O cards, four to a page, and a caller for the TV.

Print bingo cards ↑

Set up for this

Set up for a bingo night: twelve 75-ball cards, four to a page on A4. Every column has five different numbers from its own range, and the centre square is free.

Open Call the game and press Space for each ball: the call appears as B 7 or O 64, lit on a 1–75 board, and big on the TV.

The full game, with every setting, is the Bingo Card Generator.

How it works

  1. Choose 75-ball numbers, or Word bingo and type your words, one per line (at least as many as there are squares).
  2. Choose how many cards, the title and a colour; keep the fresh set code or type an old one to reprint the same cards.
  3. Press Print (1, 2 or 4 cards a page on A4 or US Letter) or Download PDF.
  4. On the day, open Call the game: each press of Next draws a number or a word fairly, lights it on the board and shows it big on the TV.

Privacy

Cards are made by your browser from the set code and, for word bingo, your own list; nothing is uploaded. Your list, your settings and the game in the caller are kept on this device so a refresh loses nothing; New game clears the caller.

Frequently asked questions

Are these the same as the bingo cards in the shops?

They follow the same 75-ball layout: B 1–15, I 16–30, N 31–45 with the free centre, G 46–60, O 61–75, five different numbers in each column.

How do I check a winning card?

Read the card number and its numbers back against the board in Call the game, which lights every number called.

What is 75-ball bingo?

The American style of bingo: a 5×5 card headed B-I-N-G-O, each column drawn from its own fifteen numbers, and a free square in the centre. Common wins are a line in any direction, four corners and a full card. (UK 90-ball tickets are a different shape: use the Tambola Ticket Generator.)

How many words do I need for word bingo?

At least as many as squares: 9 for a 3×3 card, 16 for 4×4 and 25 for 5×5, or one fewer with a free centre. With exactly that many, every card has the same words in a different order; add more words and the cards differ more.

Can I reprint the same cards?

Yes. Every card shows its set code and number. Type the code into Set code, with the same kind of card and, for word bingo, the same list in the same order, and the cards come out exactly the same.

How many cards fit on a page?

One, two or four, on A4 or US Letter. One a page is best for young children and older eyes; four a page saves paper for a big group.
