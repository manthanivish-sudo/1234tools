What is a home-made quiz?

A good home quiz has a rhythm: a question, a pause, an answer, a score. Most of the effort goes into the plumbing, such as slides, sheets and arithmetic, rather than the questions.

The page holds the quiz, shows each question on the TV, keeps your answer on your own screen, and adds up for you.

How it works in your browser

A quiz is a list of rounds of typed questions. Marking a typed answer is done by comparing words, not letters.

  1. The given answer and the real answer are lowercased, stripped of accents and punctuation, and the words “the”, “a” and “an” are dropped.
  2. A word of five to eight letters may have one slip, a word of nine or more two; shorter words must be exact.
  3. Spellings you list under Also accept count as right answers.
  4. Number questions accept a tolerance, and understand 1,066 and twenty-one alike.

Another worked example

Against the answer “The Eiffel Tower”, the typed answer “EIFFEL TOWER!” was accepted as exact. Photosynthesis typed as “photosyntesis” was accepted with one slip, and “phtosyntesis” with two, because the word has 13 letters. The answer “cats” for “cast” was refused, because a four-letter word allows no slip. With a tolerance of 5, 1,066 was accepted for 1070 and 1,080 was refused. “Twenty-one” was read as 21.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Adding a picture to a quiz you want to share by link. Links carry up to 60 questions without pictures; use Export file for a picture round.
  • Leaving a question with no note. Your note appears only on your page, so it is the place for a fact to say aloud.

More questions

How forgiving is the marking?

It ignores capitals, accents, punctuation and the words the, a and an. One slip is allowed in a word of 5 to 8 letters and two in a word of 9 or more.

Can I reorder questions?

Yes. Drag a question, or use Alt with the up or down arrow.

What stands behind each ready-made question?

Quizzes written for the site, each question with a source for its answer and a date it was last checked.

Privacy

Your quiz, its pictures and the scores stay on this device: they are kept in this browser’s own storage and shown on your own screens. Without phones, nothing is uploaded. A share link carries the quiz after the “#”, a part of a link that browsers never send to a server. Phones are optional: once you press Use phones and start a live session, each question’s words and options go through our server at live.1234tools.com to the phones, and the phones’ answers come back the same way; pictures never leave this device. Answers pass through our server only to reach the presenter’s screen. They are held in memory for the room’s life and deleted when it closes (24 hours at most). No accounts, no cookies, no analytics for participants.

How it works

  1. Type a title for the quiz in Quiz title.
  2. Choose a question type under the round (multiple choice, true or false, typed answer, number, picture or put in order) and press Add question.
  3. Type the question and its answer. For multiple choice, fill the options and press the round button beside the right one. The page saves as you type.
  4. Press Add a round for the next round. Drag a question, or press its arrows (Alt+↑ or Alt+↓), to move it.
  5. Or press Ready-made quiz and pick a quiz to start from.
  6. Press 2 Host, type the team names (one per line) and press Start the quiz.
  7. Press Show on TV and drag the new window to the TV, then press Full screen there. With one screen, press Full screen here.
  8. Press Show the question (Enter). Your page shows the answer and your note; the TV does not.
  9. Press Reveal answer (R) to show the answer on the TV.
  10. Press Right (or the team’s number key, 1 to 9) for each team that got it.
  11. Press Next (→) for the next question. Show scores (S) puts the scores on the TV.
  12. After the last question, press Final scores. To play again, press End the game, then Start the quiz.
  13. For a paper quiz, press 3 Print and choose question sheets, answer sheets or the answer key.
  14. Optional, with phones: in Host press Use phones, choose Everyone for themselves or Teams, press Start a live session, and players join at 1234tools.com/join with the 6-digit code before you press Start the quiz.

Where people use it

  • Family quiz night — four rounds for the whole family, with a picture round of baby photos
  • Pub or club quiz — printed answer sheets for the tables, the questions on the projector
  • Classroom — a ten-question recap at the end of a topic, marked on the board

Tips

  • Typed answers forgive what a quizmaster would: capitals, accents, punctuation and “the”, “a” or “an” are ignored, and one slip in a word of five letters or more (two in nine or more) still counts. Add other spellings in “Also accept”.
  • Number questions can take a tolerance (“within 5”), or tick “Closest answer wins” in Host and type each team’s guess: the nearest team gets the points.
  • Give a question a time limit and the TV counts it down, with ticks in the last five seconds and a buzzer at the end.
  • Each team may play its joker on one round to double its points there; pick it in Host before that round starts.
  • Pictures are made at most 1600 pixels across before they are kept, so a phone photo does not fill the browser’s storage.
  • A share link holds up to 60 questions without pictures. For a picture round, use Export file and send the .json: it opens with Import file on any device.
  • With phones, every question has a time limit (its own, the default you set in Host, or 20 seconds). A right answer earns the question’s points plus up to half again for speed; equal scores go to the player with the lower total time on right answers.
  • In Teams with phones, each team’s first answer from its phones is the team’s answer, so a team should agree before anyone taps. Phones choose their team when you pick Teams; anyone who does not is put in the smallest team, and you can move people under Phones and teams.

Frequently asked questions

Is the quiz uploaded anywhere?

Not unless you use phones. The questions, pictures and scores are kept in this browser’s storage on this device and shown on your own screens. Nothing is uploaded. A share link holds the quiz after the “#”, which browsers do not send to the website, and an exported file goes wherever you send it. With phones, each question’s words and options go through our server to the phones and their answers come back. Answers pass through our server only to reach the presenter’s screen. They are held in memory for the room’s life and deleted when it closes (24 hours at most). No accounts, no cookies, no analytics for participants.

Can players answer on their own phones?

Yes, if you want: phones are optional. In Host press Use phones, choose Everyone for themselves or Teams, and press Start a live session. Players go to 1234tools.com/join, type the 6-digit code (or scan the QR code on the TV) and answer with no app and no account. During the launch trial a free session takes up to 1,000 phones. At the end the TV shows the winner on a podium and each phone that answered shows its place. Export answers saves every phone’s answers, times and points as a CSV file.

How do I show the quiz on the TV?

In Host, press Show on TV to open the big-screen view in a new window, drag it to the TV or projector (or cast that browser tab) and press Full screen there. Your page stays the presenter view with the answers. With one screen, press Full screen here.

Does it mark typed answers?

If you type a team’s answer into its row, Reveal marks it: case, accents, punctuation and the words “the”, “a” and “an” are ignored, the spellings in “Also accept” count, and a small typo is forgiven in longer words. You can always change a mark by hand.

Can I make a picture round?

Yes. Choose Picture as the question type and pick a photo from the device. It is resized to at most 1600 pixels, kept in this browser, and shown big on the TV; the printed question sheet includes it too.

How do I print a pub quiz?

Open Print and choose question sheets (one set per table), answer sheets (numbered blank lines per round, with a space for the team name) or the answer key with your notes. Each opens your browser’s print dialogue, where you can also save a PDF.

Can I share the quiz with another quizmaster?

Yes. Copy share link puts a quiz of up to 60 questions without pictures into a link. For bigger quizzes or picture rounds, Export file saves a .json file (pictures included) that Import file opens on another device.

Does it work offline and on a phone?

Yes. After the first visit the page opens without a connection, and a ready-made pack you have opened once is kept too. It works on phones, tablets and computers; you can install it as an app from the browser’s menu. Playing with phones needs a connection.

Where are the ready-made packs from?

They are written for this site, each question with a source page that states the answer, and each pack shows the date it was last checked. The picker tells you if a pack has not been checked yet.
