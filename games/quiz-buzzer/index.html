What is a quiz buzzer?

Who pressed first is hard to judge by ear and impossible to judge by argument. A buzzer settles it by timing each press and ordering them, so the host announces a result instead of a guess.

This buzzer runs on one device: a tablet with a big pad for each team, or a keyboard with a key for each.

How it works in your browser

Every press carries the time stamp the browser gave it, and the winner is the earliest stamp, not the first press the page handled.

  1. A press is stored with its team and its time. The list is sorted by time, so the first entry is the winner.
  2. The gap shown beside each other team is its time minus the winner’s.
  3. Wrong locks that team out and clears the list, so the others can press again; Reset brings every team back.
  4. A team that has already pressed, or is locked out, is ignored, so the list never holds one team twice.

Another worked example

In a run with three teams, team 3 pressed at 5012.4 ms, team 1 at 5011.9 ms and team 4 at 5030 ms on the shared clock. Team 1 won, although its press was handled second, and the other two were listed 0.5 ms and 18.1 ms behind. Pressing Wrong locked team 1 out and opened the buzzer. A later press from team 1 was ignored.

Common uses

  • Family quiz night Put one tablet in the middle of the table, with a pad for each team.
  • Revision in class Let each table press for the answer; the first table to press speaks.
  • Fastest finger round Give each of four players a key on a keyboard.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Using the keyboard while a name box has focus. The keys then type into the box; click outside it first.
  • Letting two players share one key. The page keeps the first key and tells you.

More questions

Can I set a time to answer?

Yes, choose off or a time from 5 to 30 seconds. It starts at the first press and sounds a low tone at zero, but the host still decides.

Is a zero in the gap column a fault?

Browsers round time stamps slightly, so two presses within a fraction of a millisecond can show the same time.

What do the host keys do?

R resets, C marks the answer correct and W marks it wrong. They cannot be given to a team.

Privacy

Everything happens on this device. The presses are timed by your own browser and shown on your own screens; nothing is uploaded. Team names, keys and settings are remembered on this device only.

How it works

  1. Choose the number of teams and give each a name. Every team has a key and a pad; change a team’s key if you like.
  2. Read the question, then say go. The first team to press its key (or touch its pad) wins; the rest are locked out and listed with their gaps.
  3. Press Correct (C), or Wrong (W) to lock that team out and let the others try. Reset (R) opens the buzzer for the next question.
  4. Press Show on TV to put the winning team on the big screen. Tick “Send points to the scoreboard” to have Correct add points on the Quiz Scoreboard.

Where people use it

  • Family quiz night — one tablet in the middle of the table, a pad each, and the winner on the TV
  • Classroom — quick-fire revision where the first table to press answers
  • Party games — a fastest-finger round with a keyboard and four players

Tips

  • Who was first is decided by the time stamp the browser gives each press, not by the order the page happened to handle them. If a press from the TV window arrives a moment late but was made earlier, it still wins.
  • On a tablet or touch screen several teams can press their pads at the same time: each pad answers its own touch. Keep to the pads for touch; the keys work when a keyboard is attached.
  • Keys: R, C and W are the host’s Reset, Correct and Wrong, so a team cannot use them. Two teams cannot share a key; the page keeps the old key and tells you.
  • Wrong locks that team out until you press Reset and opens the buzzer for the others. Presses made by the other teams while the buzzer was locked are only listed: they do not queue, so those teams press again.
  • The answer timer (off, or 5 to 30 seconds) starts at the first press and shows on the TV. It makes a low double tone at zero; it does not decide anything, so you still press Correct or Wrong.
  • A keyboard stops working as a buzzer while you are typing in a name or key box. Click outside the boxes to play again.
  • The gap shown for each team is the time between its press and the winner’s. Browsers round time stamps a little, so differences of under a millisecond may show as 0.

Frequently asked questions

How does the buzzer know who pressed first?

Every key press and touch carries a time stamp from the browser. The page sorts the presses by those times, so the earliest one wins even when events are handled in a different order. The later teams are listed with how many milliseconds behind they were.

Can several teams use one tablet?

Yes. Each team has its own big pad and the pads respond to touch separately, so several fingers can press at once. The earliest touch wins and the others are locked out and listed.

What happens when the answer is wrong?

Press Wrong (or W). That team is locked out for the rest of the question and the buzzer opens for the others, who press again. Press Reset (or R) to bring every team back for the next question.

Can the buzzer add points to the scoreboard?

Yes. Tick “Send points to the scoreboard”, set how many points, and open the Quiz Scoreboard in the same browser. Pressing Correct sends the points to the team with the same name there. It works only between pages open in the same browser on this device.

How do I show it on the TV?

Press Show on TV to open the big-screen view in a new window, drag it to the TV or projector (or cast that browser tab) and press Full screen there. Keys pressed on the TV window’s keyboard count as presses, with their own time stamps. With one screen, press Full screen here.

Can I change the keys?

Yes. Each team has a key box: type one letter or digit. R, C and W belong to the host controls and cannot be used, and two teams cannot share a key. Your keys and names are remembered on this device.

Does it work on a phone and offline?

Yes. It works on phones, tablets and computers, and after the first visit it opens without a connection. One device is the buzzer; it is not a game for several phones at once.
