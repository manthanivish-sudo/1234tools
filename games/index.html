Made for the big screen

Every game has a big-screen view. Press Show on TV and the game opens in a second window: drag it to the TV or projector, or cast that browser tab, and press Full screen. The page you started on stays your remote. With one screen, Full screen here does the same on the device in your hand.

The controls are large enough for small fingers and older eyes, every button works from the keyboard, and the games work offline once they have opened once — useful at a party where the signal is poor. Each game can be installed as its own app from the browser’s menu.

Frequently asked questions

Is anything uploaded?

No. Names, scores and settings are handled by the page in your browser. The TV window talks to the page on the same device, not through us.

Do I need an account or an app?

No. Open the page and play. Installing a game from the browser’s menu is optional and makes it open like an app.

Are the random picks fair?

Yes. Wheels, teams and the Tambola caller draw from your browser’s cryptographic random number generator with rejection sampling, so every outcome is equally likely.

Can I use them in a school or at work?

Yes, for free, as often as you like.
