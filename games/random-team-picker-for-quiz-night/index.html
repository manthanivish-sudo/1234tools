Team Picker for a Family Quiz Night

Type everyone’s name, choose four teams, and the quiz is ready — cousins split up, fun team names, and the line-up on the TV.

Set up for this

Four teams with fun names are set by default. Add “Mum / Dad” to Keep apart so the parents are not on the same side, and mark the quiz experts with a number for balanced teams.

Keep score on the Scoreboard and use the Buzzer for the quick-fire round — both open in the same way on the TV.

The full game, with every setting, is the Random Team Generator.

How it works

  1. Type or paste the names, one per line. Add a skill after a comma (“Asha, 4”) if you want balanced teams.
  2. Choose how many teams, or how many people in each team.
  3. Pick Random, Balanced by skill, or add “Asha / Ravi” lines to keep people apart.
  4. Press Make teams. Show them on the TV, reveal them one at a time, or copy them into the family group chat.

Privacy

The names you type stay on this device. The teams are worked out by your own browser and shown on your own screens; nothing is uploaded, and the list is saved only if you tick “Keep this list on this device”.

Frequently asked questions

How many teams should a family quiz have?

Three or four teams of three to five people keeps everyone involved. Fewer, larger teams let the quiet ones hide; more, smaller ones mean long waits between turns.

Can the children be spread evenly?

Yes. Give the children a lower number (say 1) and the adults a higher one (say 3) and choose Balanced: the snake deal spreads them so each team gets a mix.

Is the split really random?

Yes. The shuffle uses your browser’s cryptographic random number generator with rejection sampling, so every arrangement is equally likely. It does not favour the names at the top of the list.

How does “balanced by skill” work?

Players are sorted by the number after their name (ties broken at random) and dealt in a snake: first pick to team 1, then 2, 3, back 3, 2, 1 and so on. The page shows each team’s total so you can see how close they are.

Can I keep brothers and sisters apart?

Yes. Add a line such as “Asha / Ravi” to the Keep apart box. The split is re-dealt until every such group is in different teams; if that is impossible (three names that must be apart but only two teams) the page tells you which line cannot be met.

How do I show the teams on the TV?

Press Show on TV to open the big-screen view in a new window, drag it to the TV or projector (or cast that browser tab) and press Full screen there. This page stays your remote. With only one screen, press Full screen here.
