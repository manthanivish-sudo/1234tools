Quiz Scoreboard

Keep score for a quiz night or a class: up to 12 teams, rounds, a joker for each team, undo and redo, a round timer and a big leaderboard for the TV. Free, no sign-up, kept on your device.

For quiz-night hosts and teachers keeping score for several teams

Scores on scrap paper get lost, argued over and added up wrongly at the very end of the night.

The usual way

  • ✗A spreadsheet nobody can read from the sofa
  • ✗Adding it all up while guests wait

What is a quiz scoreboard?

A quiz scoreboard has a small job done at the worst moment: totals must be right when the room is loud and someone wants the standings now. Paper and mental arithmetic are where the arguments start.

This one keeps every point in a table of teams and rounds, so a total is always the sum of what you can see.

How it works in your browser

The state is one small record of teams, rounds and points, and every button changes it in a way that can be undone.

  1. Points are added to the team in the round shown as Scoring round, and a total is the sum of that team’s rounds.
  2. A joker marks one round for a team and doubles that round’s points when the totals are worked out, minus points included.
  3. Ranks count how many teams have a higher total, so equal totals share a rank and the next rank is skipped.
  4. Every change is saved in the browser as it happens, and Undo and Redo walk back and forward through up to 300 steps.

Another worked example

With four teams, adding 5 points to Team 1 and playing its joker in round 1 gave it 10, and adding 7 to Team 2 gave 7. After a second round, with 4 more for Team 1 and 3 for Team 3, the totals were 14, 7, 3 and 0. Totals of 14, 7, 7 and 3 were ranked 1, 2, 2 and 4. The CSV file opened with the columns Rank, Team, Round 1, Round 2, Joker round and Total. The page held a game to 12 teams, even when asked for 20.

Common uses

  • Pub or club quiz Put up to 12 teams and their round scores on one screen.
  • House points in school Keep a week of points on the teacher’s laptop and show the standings on Friday.
  • Family game night Let the youngest hold the keyboard and enter the points.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Scoring into the wrong round. Check the Scoring round list before you add points, or pick the earlier round to correct it.
  • Forgetting that a joker is for one round only. Playing it again in a later round is refused.

More questions

Why do two teams show the same rank?

Equal totals share a rank. Totals of 14, 7, 7 and 3 are ranked 1, 2, 2 and 4, as in most sports tables.

Does a negative score count against a joker?

Yes, the joker doubles minus points as well as plus points in the round where it is played.

Is the CSV file the same as the table?

It has one row for each team with the rank, every round, the joker round and the total, with jokers already doubled.

Privacy

The team names and scores stay on this device. They are kept in this browser so a refresh does not lose the game, and nothing is uploaded; “New game” clears the scores.

How it works

  1. Choose how many teams are playing and type their names. Each team has a colour; the name is always shown too.
  2. Press +1, +5 or −1 for a team, or type any number of points and press Add. Press Next round when the round is over.
  3. Play a joker for a team to double its points in that round. Use Undo (U) if you slip.
  4. Press Show on TV for a big leaderboard that re-orders as the scores change. Export the results as a CSV file when the quiz ends.

Where people use it

  • Family quiz night — four teams, six rounds, a joker each, and the leaderboard on the TV
  • Classroom — points for tables or houses across the week, kept on the teacher’s device
  • Pub or club quiz — up to 12 teams with the totals and the round-by-round scores in one table

Tips

  • Keys: 1 to 9 pick a team, + and − change the picked team by one point, U undoes and Y redoes. They work when no box is being typed in, and on the TV window’s keyboard too.
  • A joker doubles that team’s points in the round it is played, including minus points. Press the joker button again in the same round to take it back. A played joker can be taken back only while you are scoring the round it was played in.
  • Points always go into the round shown in “Scoring round”. To fix an earlier round, choose it from the list; new points go there until you press Next round.
  • Equal totals share a rank: 14, 7, 7 and 3 points are ranked 1st, 2nd, 2nd and 4th. On the TV, ties keep the order of the teams on this page.
  • The timer counts down from the clock, so it stays right even if the page is busy. It makes a buzz at zero but does not stop the scoring.
  • The game is saved on this device after every change, so a refresh or a closed tab does not lose it. “New game” asks first, keeps the team names and colours, and clears the scores and rounds.
  • With the Quiz Buzzer open in the same browser, tick its “send points” box and a correct answer adds points here, to the team with the same name, in the current round.

Frequently asked questions

How does the joker work?

Each team has one joker. Playing it doubles that team’s points in the round you are scoring, and the table marks the round with ×2. Playing it again in the same round takes it back; while you are scoring a different round a played joker cannot be moved.

Can I undo a mistake?

Yes. Undo (or the U key) takes back the last change and Redo (Y) puts it back. Points, jokers, new rounds, team changes and a new game can all be undone, up to 300 steps back. Closing the page forgets the undo list but keeps the scores.

How do I show the scores on the TV?

Press Show on TV to open the leaderboard in a new window, drag it to the TV or projector (or cast that browser tab) and press Full screen there. This page stays your remote. With one screen, press Full screen here. The ranks re-order smoothly whenever the scores change.

Will I lose the scores if I refresh the page?

No. The game is saved in your browser after every change and comes back when you reopen the page on the same device. It is not sent anywhere, so it will not appear on another device.

Can I get the results as a spreadsheet?

Yes. Export CSV downloads a file with one row per team: rank, team, each round’s score (jokers already doubled), the joker round and the total. It opens in any spreadsheet.

How many teams and rounds can I have?

From 2 to 12 teams and up to 50 rounds. Changing the number of teams keeps the teams that remain; teams removed from the end take their scores with them, and Undo brings them back.

Can the buzzer add the points for me?

Yes, when the Quiz Buzzer and this scoreboard are open in the same browser. In the buzzer, tick “Send points to the scoreboard”; pressing Correct then adds the points to the team of the same name here, in the current round.
