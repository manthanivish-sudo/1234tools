Quiz Night with Phones

Live — answers pass through our server

Two rounds of timed questions on the big screen, a leaderboard after each, and a podium for the winners.

  • Free
  • One code to join

Why people use it

For families, quiz-night hosts and community groups with a TV

Quiz night means paper answer sheets, a host who marks them all, and the scores arriving an hour late.

The usual way

  • ✗Paper answer sheets collected and marked
  • ✗Shouting answers across the room
  • ✗A host adding up scores on a napkin

With this tool

✓Faster right answers score more; a podium finish with confetti.

Host a quiz night ↑

Set up for this

This page starts with a quiz-night deck: two rounds of timed questions, each followed by a leaderboard; after the last question the winner is announced on a podium. Answers are revealed as soon as the time runs out or everyone has answered.

Players join at 1234tools.com/join with the code on the TV. After each question the TV shows the top 5 and every phone shows its own points, answering time, total and place; equal points go to the faster player.

The full tool, with every setting, is the Live Presentation.

How it works

  1. The sample deck is ready to try. Press a slide in “Your slides” to change it, or pick a type under “Add a slide” and press Add.
  2. For each question type the text and the answers, one per line. Tick the correct answer if there is one.
  3. Press “Start a live session”. A 6-digit code and a QR code appear.
  4. Press “Show on TV” and drag the new window to the projector, then press Full screen there.
  5. Tell the audience: open 1234tools.com/join on your phone (or scan the QR code), type the code, pick a nickname and press Join.
  6. Press “Present”. The first slide appears on the big screen and on every phone.
  7. Press Next (→ or Space) to move on and Previous (←) to go back. Press R to reveal the results, H to hide them and C to close or reopen voting. On the last slide Next becomes Finish: the big screen and every phone show the finale, with the winner and confetti after a quiz.
  8. Press “Export CSV” to save every slide’s results, then “End session” (press it twice) to close the room on every phone.

Privacy

Answers pass through our server only to reach the presenter’s screen. They are held in memory for the room’s life and deleted when it closes (24 hours at most). No accounts, no cookies, no analytics for participants.

Frequently asked questions

How do points work?

A right answer scores between 1000 (straight away) and 500 (at the buzzer); a wrong answer scores 0. The leaderboard adds up every quiz question before it.

Can teams play?

Yes: one phone per team, with the team name as the nickname.

Do people need an app or an account?

No. They open 1234tools.com/join in their phone’s browser, type the 6-digit code (or scan the QR code) and choose a nickname. You need no account either.

Do phones change slide by themselves?

Yes. When you press Next or Previous, every joined phone switches to that slide at once, without reloading. A content slide shows “Look at the screen”.

Can I hide the answers until I am ready?

Yes. Multiple-choice and quiz slides start with “Hide results until I reveal” ticked: the screen shows only how many have answered until you press R. Then the counts, percentages and the correct answers appear on the screen and on the phones.

How are quiz points worked out?

A right answer scores 1000 points if it arrives at once, falling evenly to 500 at the deadline; a wrong answer or no answer scores 0. The time is measured by our relay when the answer arrives, so a slow phone clock makes no difference. Players on equal points are ranked by the total time their right answers took, fastest first.
