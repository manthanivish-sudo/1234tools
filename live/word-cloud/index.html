Live Word Cloud

Live — answers pass through our server

Ask the room for a word and watch a live word cloud build on the projector as phones answer at 1234tools.com/join. Words grouped, rude words masked, any word hidden in a tap. Free, no sign-up, no app.

Privacy

Answers pass through our server only to reach the presenter’s screen. They are held in memory for the room’s life and deleted when it closes (24 hours at most). No accounts, no cookies, no analytics for participants.

How it works

  1. Type the prompt, for example “One word for this year”, and choose how many words each phone may send (1, 2 or 3).
  2. Press “Start a live session”. A 6-digit code and a QR code appear.
  3. Press “Show on TV” and drag the new window to the projector, then press Full screen there.
  4. Tell the audience: open 1234tools.com/join (or scan the QR code), type the code, pick a nickname and press Join.
  5. Press “Ask for words”. Each phone types a word and presses Send.
  6. Watch the cloud grow. Press Hide next to any word you do not want on screen; press Close (C) when you have enough.
  7. Press “Export CSV” to save the words and counts, then “End session” (press it twice).

Step by step

Before people arrive

  1. Open Live Word Cloud on the laptop connected to the projector.
  2. Type the prompt and choose 1, 2 or 3 words per phone.
  3. Leave “Mask rude words” ticked unless you have a reason not to.
  4. Press “Start a live session”: a 6-digit code and a QR code appear.

Running it

  1. Audience: open 1234tools.com/join on a phone or scan the QR code.
  2. Audience: type the code, choose a nickname and press Join.
  3. Presenter: press “Ask for words”.
  4. Audience: type a word (up to 25 characters) and press Send; repeat up to the limit.
  5. Presenter: press Hide next to any word to take it off the big screen; press Close (C) to stop answers.
  6. Presenter: Export CSV, then End session (press twice).

On the projector or TV

  1. Press “Show on TV (new window)” and drag that window to the projector or TV.
  2. Press Full screen in that window; this page stays your remote.
  3. With one screen only, press “Full screen here”; press Esc to come back.
  4. Untick “Show the join code on the big screen” to give the cloud the whole screen.

If something goes wrong

A word shows up twice.

Words are grouped ignoring capitals and spaces, but different spellings (“colour”, “color”) stay apart. Hide one if you wish.

A rude word got through.

Press Hide next to it on this page; it leaves the big screen and the phones at once.

Phones cannot send any more words.

Each phone may send the number of words you chose. Ask again with a new prompt for another round.

The cloud is too crowded.

The big screen shows the 60 most common words. Hide the ones you do not need, or untick the join code strip for more room.

Where people use it

  • Team meeting — one word for how the quarter went, seen by everyone at once
  • Classroom — what pupils already know about a topic before the lesson
  • Workshop — a brainstorm where the most common ideas grow biggest

Tips

  • Words are grouped ignoring capitals and spaces: “Team Work”, “teamwork” and “TEAMWORK” count as one word.
  • Each phone counts once per word, so one enthusiastic person cannot make a word grow by sending it again.
  • No two words ever overlap: each word is placed on a spiral from the centre at the first spot where it fits, the most common first.
  • Rude words are masked to their first letter and stars, in English and Hindi or Hinglish, from a list kept in this page. Untick the box to show them as typed.
  • Hide takes a word off the big screen and off the phones at once; Show brings it back.
  • Tick “Show top answers on phones” to let the audience see the top twenty words on their own screens too.

Frequently asked questions

Do people need an app or an account?

No. They open 1234tools.com/join in their phone’s browser, type the 6-digit code (or scan the QR code) and choose a nickname.

How many people can take part?

A free session takes up to 100 phones at once. Each phone can send one, two or three words, as you choose.

How are rude words handled?

Masked by default: a list of rude words in English and Hindi or Hinglish is kept in the page, and a match is shown as its first letter and stars. Look-alike spellings (sh!t, b1tch) and stretched ones are caught too. No list is perfect, so you can also hide any word by hand.

Can people see who wrote which word?

No. The cloud shows words and counts only. Phones never receive another person’s answers; with “Show top answers on phones” they see the top twenty words and their counts.

What does the server keep?

The relay at live.1234tools.com passes the prompt to the phones and each answer to you. It keeps only the current prompt in memory for late joiners and forgets everything when the session ends (24 hours at most). The words are counted on your device.

How long can an answer be?

Up to 25 characters, so a short phrase works (“more coffee”). Longer answers are refused on the phone.

Can I save the cloud?

Export CSV saves every word with its count. For a picture, take a screenshot of the big-screen window.
