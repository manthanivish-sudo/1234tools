How a live session works

  1. Open a live tool on the computer connected to the projector and type your question.
  2. Press Start a live session. A 6-digit code and a QR code appear; press Show on TV to put them on the big screen.
  3. The audience open 1234tools.com/join on their phones (or scan the QR code), type the code and choose a nickname.
  4. Ask the question. Answers arrive on your screen in about a second and the results build up live.
  5. Export the results as a CSV, then press End session: every phone is told and the room is gone.

What our server sees and keeps

Answers pass through our server only to reach the presenter’s screen. They are held in memory for the room’s life and deleted when it closes (24 hours at most). No accounts, no cookies, no analytics for participants.

These are the only free tools on 1234Tools whose answers leave the device, so here is exactly what happens. A small relay at live.1234tools.com connects the presenter’s browser with the phones. It passes each answer to the presenter and the presenter’s question to the phones, and keeps the current question in memory so that someone who joins late sees it. It writes nothing to disk. When the presenter ends the session — or after 24 hours, or 30 minutes after the presenter’s page goes away — the room and everything in it is deleted.

All the counting is done in the presenter’s browser, one answer per phone. Phones receive the question and, only if the presenter shows them, the totals — never another person’s answer. Participants choose a nickname, not their real name; there are no accounts, no cookies and no analytics on the join page. A free session takes up to 100 phones.

Frequently asked questions

Do participants need an app?

No. Any phone browser works: open 1234tools.com/join, type the code (or scan the QR code on the screen) and pick a nickname.

Do I need an account to run a session?

No. Open a live tool and press Start a live session. Nothing is sent anywhere until you press it.

How many people can join?

Up to 100 phones in a free session. The 101st phone is told the session is full.

What happens to the answers afterwards?

They stay on the presenter’s page until it is closed, and can be exported as a CSV from there. The relay deletes the room when the session ends; nothing is kept by us.

Can I use it in a video call?

Yes. Share the big-screen window in the call; people answer from their phones or from another browser tab.
