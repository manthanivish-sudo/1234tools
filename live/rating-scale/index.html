Live Rating Scale

Live — answers pass through our server

Let the audience rate a talk, an idea or how well they understood, from their phones: stars, 0 to 10 or a slider. The average, the spread and a histogram fill in live on the big screen. Free, no sign-up.

Privacy

Answers pass through our server only to reach the presenter’s screen. They are held in memory for the room’s life and deleted when it closes (24 hours at most). No accounts, no cookies, no analytics for participants.

How it works

  1. Type what people should rate, for example “How useful was today’s session?”.
  2. Choose the scale: 1 to 5 stars, 0 to 10 buttons, or a 0 to 10 slider. Add words for each end if you like (“Not at all”, “Very”).
  3. Press “Start a live session”. A 6-digit code and a QR code appear.
  4. Press “Show on TV” and drag the new window to the projector, then press Full screen there.
  5. Tell the audience: open 1234tools.com/join (or scan the QR code), type the code, pick a nickname and press Join.
  6. Press “Ask for ratings”. Each phone taps a star or a number (or moves the slider and presses Send rating).
  7. Watch the average and the histogram. Press “Reveal results” (R) if you hid them, and “Close” (C) when done.
  8. Press “Export CSV” to save the ratings, then “End session” (press it twice).

Step by step

Before people arrive

  1. Open Live Rating Scale on the laptop connected to the projector.
  2. Type the question and choose stars, 0 to 10 or a slider.
  3. Optionally type words for the low and high ends.
  4. Press “Start a live session”: a 6-digit code and a QR code appear.

Running it

  1. Audience: open 1234tools.com/join on a phone or scan the QR code, type the code, choose a nickname and press Join.
  2. Presenter: press “Ask for ratings”.
  3. Audience: tap a star or a number, or move the slider and press Send rating. Tap again to change it.
  4. Presenter: press Reveal results (R) if hidden, then Close (C).
  5. Presenter: Export CSV, then End session (press twice).

On the projector or TV

  1. Press “Show on TV (new window)” and drag that window to the projector or TV.
  2. Press Full screen in that window; this page stays your remote.
  3. With one screen only, press “Full screen here”; press Esc to come back.

If something goes wrong

The average changed after someone rated twice.

Each phone has one rating; a second tap replaces the first, so the average moves to match.

Phones still show the old question.

Press “Ask for ratings” again after changing the question: phones get the new one at once.

I want the room not to see the results yet.

Tick “Hide results until I press Reveal” before asking; the big screen then shows only how many have rated.

Where people use it

  • Training — rate each session out of 5 before the coffee break
  • Classroom — how well did you understand this, 0 to 10, before moving on
  • Product team — how much would customers want this idea, on a slider from “meh” to “take my money”

Tips

  • One rating per phone: rating again replaces the earlier one, so changing your mind never counts twice.
  • The spread is the standard deviation: a small spread means the room agrees, a large one that opinions are split even if the average looks middling.
  • The histogram shows every value, so a room split between 2 and 9 does not hide behind an average of 5.5.
  • Words for the ends (“Not at all” and “Completely”) appear on every phone under the scale.
  • Results on phones stay off until you reveal them; untick “Show results on phones too” to keep them on the big screen only.
  • Export CSV saves the count for every value of every question, with the average and the spread, made in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Do people need an app or an account?

No. They open 1234tools.com/join in their phone’s browser, type the 6-digit code (or scan the QR code) and choose a nickname.

Which scales can I use?

One to five stars, 0 to 10 as eleven buttons, or a 0 to 10 slider. Each can have words at the low and high ends.

How is the average worked out?

It is the mean of everyone’s latest rating, shown to one decimal place. The spread is the standard deviation of the same ratings.

Can people see each other’s ratings?

No. Phones receive only the question and, if you show them, the average, the count and the histogram — never who gave which rating.

How many people can rate?

A free session takes up to 100 phones at once.

What does the server keep?

The relay at live.1234tools.com passes the question to the phones and each rating to you. It keeps only the current question in memory for late joiners and forgets everything when the session ends (24 hours at most). Ratings are counted on your device.

Can I ask several questions?

Yes. Change the question and press Ask for ratings again; the earlier results are kept and all of them go into the CSV export.
