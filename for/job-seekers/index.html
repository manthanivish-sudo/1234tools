Frequently asked questions

Is my CV sent anywhere?

Only by the CV screener, which is an AI tool and says so at the top of its page. It masks your email, phone number and ID numbers on your device before anything is sent, shows you what will go, and nothing is stored after the answer comes back. Every other tool here runs entirely in your browser.

Will the passport photo be accepted?

The tool lays out the photo at the official size for the country you pick and prints it as a sheet. Acceptance still depends on the photo itself — lighting, expression, a plain background — so read the checklist on the page before you print, and check the authority’s current rules.

My offer letter gives a CTC. What will I actually get?

Use the CTC to take-home calculator for India, or the UK take-home pay calculator for a gross salary in the UK. Both show the deductions line by line, so you can see where the difference between the headline and your account goes.
