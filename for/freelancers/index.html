Frequently asked questions

Am I caught by Making Tax Digital?

If you are self-employed or a landlord it depends on your qualifying income — which is gross, before a single expense, added up across everything you do. That is the part people get wrong. The checker works it out and tells you which band you are in and from when.

Do I need an account for any of this?

Only for the AI tools. Everything that runs in the browser — the documents, the calculators, the reconciliations — needs nothing at all.

Can I use the invoice generator commercially?

Yes. The documents are yours; there is no watermark, no branding of ours on them and no limit on how many you make.
