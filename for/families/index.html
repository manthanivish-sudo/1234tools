Frequently asked questions

Does anything I enter get uploaded?

Not by the games, calculators and photo tools: they run in your browser, so names, scores, loan figures and photos stay on your device. The live tools are the exception, because phones have to reach the screen somehow. The privacy statement for them is: Answers pass through our server only to reach the presenter’s screen. They are held in memory for the room’s life and deleted when it closes (24 hours at most). No accounts, no cookies, no analytics for participants.

Do the children need an app or an account?

No. The games run on one laptop or phone, with the TV showing the board. For a live poll the family open 1234tools.com/join in a browser and type a six-digit code. During the launch trial a free session takes up to 1,000 phones.

Are the loan and savings calculators financial advice?

No. They do the arithmetic on the figures you enter. A bank’s own quote includes fees and rate changes that a general calculator cannot know, so use the result to compare options and ask the lender for the exact figures.

Can I make a quiz in Hindi or another language?

The Quiz Maker shows the questions you type. The Antakshari scorekeeper draws its letter in Hindi or English, and the Tambola caller has Hindi calls, which need a Hindi text-to-speech voice on the device to be spoken.
