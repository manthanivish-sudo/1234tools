Frequently asked questions

What do the reminder letters say?

They list the customer’s overdue invoices with dates, amounts and how many days late each is, then a total, then what you would like to happen. The tone steps up on its own — a note at a fortnight, a firmer letter at six weeks, a final one after that — and none of them threatens legal action. They are drafts with your name on them; read them before sending.

Can I charge interest on a late payment?

In the UK there is a statutory right to interest and a fixed sum on a late commercial payment, and the page links to the gov.uk guidance. The tool does not calculate it, because whether you are entitled and at what rate depends on your contract — but whatever terms line you type is printed under every letter.
