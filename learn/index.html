What this is

The rest of this site is tools that run on your own device. This part is different: it is a reading list, and every link on it leads somewhere else.

It is kept short on purpose. Anyone can paste five hundred links; the useful thing is knowing which ten to open first and why, so each subject opens with that and each entry says what it actually is, who made it, and what it costs before you click.

Nothing here is sponsored and no link pays us a penny. Primary sources are preferred — universities, standards bodies, the people who wrote the language — because those go stale slowest. Everything is re-checked automatically every week, so a dead link gets found by us rather than by you.
