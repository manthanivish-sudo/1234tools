Practice, Mocks & Interviews

Problem sets, contests and mock interviews, for keeping sharp or getting hired.

Where to start

For interviews, a curated list beaten thoroughly beats a thousand problems attempted once — NeetCode exists for exactly that reason. Contests are a different skill from interviews; do them because they are enjoyable, not because you think they are the same practice. System design is the part most people under-prepare and the part senior interviews turn on.
