Certifications & Exam Prep

Official certification paths, and honest notes on which are worth paying for.

Where to start

A certificate is worth what the hiring market pays for it, which varies enormously — cloud and security certifications clear that bar, most others do not. Always prepare from the official exam guide rather than a course description, because the guide is what the questions are written against. Everything listed here is the vendor’s own page, so the syllabus is the current one.

  • AWS CertificationPaid

    Exam guides, sample questions and the official path from Cloud Practitioner upwards. The guides are free even though the exams are not.

    Amazon Web Servicesaws.amazon.com

  • Microsoft CredentialsPaid

    Azure, Security and Power Platform certifications, each with a free structured learning path attached.

    Microsoftlearn.microsoft.com

  • Google Cloud CertificationPaid

    Associate and Professional tracks. The Professional Cloud Architect case studies are published and worth reading early.

    Google Cloudcloud.google.com

  • CompTIA CertificationsPaid

    Vendor-neutral entry points — A+, Network+, Security+. Security+ is the one that opens doors in defensive security.

    CompTIAcomptia.org

  • Certified Kubernetes AdministratorPaid

    Entirely hands-on: a real cluster and a time limit. One of the few certifications that cannot be passed by memorising.

    CNCFcncf.io

  • Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)Free tier

    An entry-level security certification that ISC2 has been offering free of charge to newcomers, training included.

    ISC2isc2.org

  • Cisco Networking AcademyFree tier

    Free networking and security courses, including the CCNA preparation material.

    Cisconetacad.com

  • Project Management InstitutePaid

    CAPM and PMP. Worth it in organisations that ask for it by name, and rarely otherwise.

    PMIpmi.org
