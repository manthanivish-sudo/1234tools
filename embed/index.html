Embed our calculators

Any calculator or unit converter on 1234Tools can go on your own page: copy two lines of HTML and the tool works there, for your readers, exactly as it does here. Free, no account, nothing your readers type is sent anywhere, and a small “Powered by 1234Tools” credit underneath.

72 calculators and 1,048 unit converters can be embedded · free · no account · no analytics inside the frame

Every calculator and converter on this site has an Embed button in its share bar. It writes an iframe that shows the tool on its own — no header, no sidebar, no footer — and a short paragraph under it that links back to the tool. Paste both into your page and you are done.

This page explains what that frame does and does not do, shows two of them working, and gets you the code for any of the 1,120 without opening each one.

Why put one on your page

  • Free, and nothing to sign up to. There is no key, no account and no plan. Paste the code and it works.
  • Nothing your readers type is sent anywhere. The sums are done in their own browser. The frame loads from 1234tools.com and asks nothing of any other site; the currency converter fetches the day’s rates from this site too.
  • No analytics inside the frame. With ?embed=1 in the address there is no consent banner, no Google Analytics and no Clarity. Nothing asks your readers to accept anything.
  • Just the tool. The header, the sidebar, the footer, the share bar and the explanations are hidden. What is left is the tool, its answer and a one-line credit with a link to the full page.
  • No adverts. There is no advertising code on these pages, so nothing in the frame competes with yours.
  • It keeps working once it has loaded. Every answer after that is worked out in the reader’s browser, without another trip to the network.

What the code looks like

This is the code for the BMI calculator. Every tool’s code has the same two parts.

<iframe src="https://www.1234tools.com/health/bmi/?embed=1&amp;utm_source=embed&amp;utm_medium=share&amp;utm_campaign=health&amp;utm_content=bmi" width="100%" height="900" style="border:0;border-radius:12px;max-width:720px" loading="lazy" title="BMI Calculator — 1234Tools"></iframe>
<p style="font:14px system-ui"><a href="https://www.1234tools.com/health/bmi/">BMI Calculator</a> by 1234Tools — free, runs in the browser.</p>
  • The iframe. Its address ends in ?embed=1, which is what hides the site around the tool. The utm_ parameters say which tool it is; they carry nothing about you or your readers.
  • width="100%" and max-width:720px. The frame fills your column, up to 720 pixels.
  • loading="lazy". The frame loads only when a reader scrolls near it, so it does not slow the top of your page.
  • The paragraph. A plain link to the tool, in your readers’ system font so it sits quietly in your page.

A converter’s table of every unit is left out of embeds, so a converter fits its height. To show more without scrolling, raise the height.
  • Too much space? If there is empty space under the credit on your page, lower the height until it goes.
  • Narrow columns make it a little taller. On a phone, or in a sidebar, labels and answers wrap onto more lines. Check your page at phone width before you settle on a height.
  • Leave the width at 100%. The tool is built to fit any width from a phone up, so a fixed pixel width only gets in its way.

The one thing we ask: keep the credit

Use the code on a blog, a school or club site, or a small business page — that is what it is for. Keep the paragraph under the frame and its link to the tool: restyle it to suit your page if you like, but leave the link in. It is how anybody ever finds this site, and it is the whole of the price.

Inside the frame there is a “Powered by 1234Tools” line and an “Open the full tool” link. They are part of the tool and stay whatever the code around them says. Please do not present the tool as your own.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything?

No. There is no account, no key and no plan behind the embed code, and nothing to renew. Paste it into your page and it works.

Does it track my readers?

No analytics run inside the frame. When a tool is opened with ?embed=1 in its address there is no consent banner, no Google Analytics and no Clarity: the analytics script stops before it loads anything. The frame is fetched from 1234tools.com like any other page, so the request reaches our host the way a request for your page reaches yours.

Can I make it match my site’s colours?

Not at the moment. The frame shows the tool in this site’s own dark theme whatever your page looks like; there is no setting for a light version or for your colours. The rounded corners and the border in the code are yours to change.

Which tools can be embedded?

Every calculator and every unit converter — 1,120 of them. The PDF, image and AI tools have no embed code: they need a file chooser, a download or an account, which belong on their own pages. Link to those instead.

Will it work on a phone?

Yes. The frame is 100% wide and the tool rearranges itself to fit the column, which makes it a little taller on a narrow screen. Look at your page on a phone, and raise the height in the code if more of the tool should show without scrolling inside the frame.

Can the frame open with figures already filled in?

Yes. Open the tool on this site, enter the figures, tick “Include my figures” in its share bar, then press Embed. The figures travel after the # in the frame’s address, so the tool opens showing that answer — and your readers can still change it. The picker on this page writes the code without figures.
