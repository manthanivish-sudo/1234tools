What is a ratio?

A ratio compares the sizes of two amounts: 3 : 4 means for every 3 of the first there are 4 of the second. It fixes the proportion, not the quantities, so 30 : 40 and 300 : 400 are the same ratio.

Mixes, shares and scale drawings are specified as ratios because the proportion stays put while the quantity changes.

How it is calculated

Simplifying divides both terms by their greatest common divisor. A proportion A : B = C : D is solved by cross-multiplying. Sharing a total gives each side its own part of A + B.

Another worked example

Two partners put in £5,000 and £3,000, a ratio of 5 : 3, and the year’s profit is £1,200. Enter A = 5, B = 3 and share a total of 1200: A takes £750 and B £450, which is 62.5% and 37.5%. With C = 15, the proportion 5 : 3 = 15 : D gives D = 9, so a later £15,000 stake on A’s side would be matched by £9,000 on B’s.

Common uses

  • Scale drawings and maps At 1 : 50, 9 cm on the drawing is 450 cm, or 4.5 m, on site.
  • Bike gearing A 48-tooth chainring driving a 16-tooth sprocket simplifies to 3 : 1, three wheel turns per turn of the pedals.
  • Dilution A 1 : 4 squash or a 1 : 3 mortar keeps its strength as the batch grows.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Rounding decimal terms before entering them. 1.5 : 2 rounded to 2 : 2 becomes 1 : 1; entered as it is, it is scaled to whole numbers and simplified to 3 : 4.
  • Putting C on the wrong side. C has to correspond to A; if your known amount belongs with B, swap A and B first.

More questions

How do I share an amount in a ratio?

Add the parts, divide the amount by that total, then multiply by each part. £900 shared 2 : 7 is £900 ÷ 9 = £100 a part, so £200 and £700.

How do I simplify a ratio?

Divide both terms by the largest number that goes into each. 45 : 60 shares a factor of 15 and becomes 3 : 4.

How do I write a ratio as a percentage?

Put each term over the total of both and multiply by 100. In a class of girls to boys at 3 : 2, girls are 3 ÷ 5 = 60% and boys 40%.

Can a ratio have three parts?

Yes, such as 1 : 2 : 3 for a concrete mix, but this calculator takes two terms. For a three-way split, add the parts to 6 and give each 1/6, 2/6 and 3/6 of the total.

Formula

a : b = c : d  →  d = bc / a

Tips

  • A ratio compares parts to each other; a fraction compares a part to the whole. In 3:4, A is 3/7 of the total, not 3/4.
  • Scaling a recipe or a mix is a proportion problem: keep A:B fixed and solve for the new quantity.
  • Aspect ratios are just ratios in their simplest form — 1920:1080 reduces to 16:9.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a ratio and a rate?

A ratio compares two quantities of the same kind and has no units. A rate compares different kinds — miles per hour, cost per unit — and carries units.
