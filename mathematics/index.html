Frequently asked questions

Do the calculators show the steps?

The quadratic solver and the fraction and percentage calculators show the formula they used and the values along the way, so an answer can be checked rather than copied.

Which standard deviation does the statistics calculator give?

Both. Sample and population standard deviation are shown side by side, with the variance for each.

Can I use these in an exam?

That depends on your exam board, not on us. They are for learning and checking; a board that bans calculators bans these too.
