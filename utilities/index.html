Frequently asked questions

What does the Tool Finder do?

You describe the job in plain words and it names the tool to use, from everything on the site. It runs on your device: the list of tools is searched by your browser and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Are the random numbers really random?

They come from your browser’s cryptographic random source, which is suitable for draws and passwords. The dice roller uses the same source.

Why does cups to grams depend on the ingredient?

Because a cup is a volume and ingredients differ in density. The converter uses a weight per cup for each ingredient on its list, which is why flour and sugar give different grams.
