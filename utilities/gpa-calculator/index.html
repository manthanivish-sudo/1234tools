What is a GPA?

A grade point average turns letter grades into one number by giving each grade a point value, from 4.0 for an A down to 0 for an F, and averaging them. US colleges and many graduate programmes ask for it, and Indian universities report the similar CGPA on a 10-point scale.

When courses carry different credits the average is weighted, so a heavy module pulls the result harder than a short one.

How it is calculated

Each grade becomes points, and points times credits gives quality points. Total quality points divided by total credits is the GPA on the 4.0 scale; the 10- and 5-point results are straight multiples of it.

GPA (4.0) = Σ(points × credits) ÷ Σ creditsGPA (10) = GPA (4.0) × 2.5 GPA (5) = GPA (4.0) × 1.25percentage = GPA (4.0) ÷ 4 × 100

Another worked example

A term has four courses: an A in a 2-credit seminar, an A− and a B in two 3-credit courses, and a C+ in a 6-credit lab. The quality points are 8 + 11.1 + 9 + 13.8 = 41.9 over 14 credits, a GPA of 2.99. Leave the credits box empty and the same grades average 3.25, because the lab then counts no more than the seminar.

Common uses

  • Applications Graduate schools, scholarships and some employers set a minimum GPA.
  • Exchange and transfer forms Restate a 4.0-scale GPA on a 10-point scale, or the reverse, as a first estimate.
  • Planning next term Enter the grades you expect to see how far a strong term can lift the figure.
  • Checking a transcript Confirm the GPA a registrar printed from your own grades and credits.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Giving fewer credits than grades. With grades A, B, C and credits 3, 3 the tool notes “You gave 2 credit values for 3 grades” and averages without weights.
  • Averaging semester GPAs directly. A 3.8 over 12 credits and a 3.0 over 18 credits make 3.32, not 3.4; enter the two GPAs as grades with their credits to weight them.

More questions

What is a 3.5 GPA as a percentage?

On this tool’s proportional conversion, 87.5%. Universities that publish their own table may give a different figure.

How do I convert a GPA to the 10-point scale?

Choose the 10.0 scale and enter the grades or the GPA itself: 3.2 on the 4.0 scale becomes 8.0.

What is a quality point?

A grade’s points multiplied by its credits. A B+ in a 4-credit course earns 13.2 quality points.

Does an F count in a GPA?

Yes, as 0 points with its full credits. An A and an F in two 3-credit courses average 2.0.

Formula

GPA = Σ(grade points × credits) / Σ credits

Tips

  • Give credits to weight by course size. Without them every course counts equally, which usually understates a heavy module.
  • Grade-to-point mappings differ between institutions, particularly for A+ and for pass/fail courses. Check your handbook.
  • Scale conversion here is proportional. Many institutions publish their own conversion table, which will not always match.

Frequently asked questions

Is the percentage conversion official?

No. Percentage-to-GPA mappings vary by country and institution, and some use conversion tables rather than a straight proportion. Use the figure as a rough indication and quote your official transcript for applications.
