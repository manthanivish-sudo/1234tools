Mute Video

Remove the sound from a video in your browser. The picture is copied untouched into a new MP4 or WebM, so the quality stays exactly the same and a long video takes seconds. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Free
  • Nothing uploaded
  • Picture copied untouched

Why people use it

For people posting clips with sound they cannot or should not share

Background music gets posts blocked, and what was said behind the camera was not meant for everyone.

The usual way

  • ✗Turning the volume to zero in an app (the sound is still there)
  • ✗Re-exporting the whole video from an editor
  • ✗Uploading it to an online editor

With this tool

✓The picture is copied into a new file untouched; the sound track is removed, not turned down.

Mute a video ↑

What is muting a video?

Muting here means removing the sound track itself, not turning it down. The new file holds the picture alone, so no player, editor or social site can bring the old sound back.

Because the picture does not need to change, it does not need to be decoded either: its stored frames are copied into a new container exactly as they were.

How it works in your browser

The page reads the file’s index, keeps the picture track’s list of samples and writes a new container around them.

  1. The samples are read from your disk in batches of up to 4 MB and written into an MP4 (for MP4, MOV and MKV files) or a WebM (for WebM files).
  2. Each frame keeps its own timestamp and keyframe flag, so seeking works as before.
  3. A phone video’s rotation flag is carried over, so an upright video stays upright.
  4. Only a picture codec the new container cannot hold is re-encoded, and the page says so before you start.

Another worked example

The 6-second test clip of 198.7 KB came out as clip-muted.mp4 at 102.8 KB: the same picture, played back frame for frame, and no sound track at all. The difference is almost exactly what its 128 kbit/s sound took up.

Common uses

  • Product demos Silent clips autoplay on most web pages and in social feeds without being blocked.
  • Classroom screens Show a clip without its commentary while you talk over it.
  • Before adding a voice-over Start from a clean picture so the old sound does not leak through.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Muting the original by mistake. The tool never changes your file; it saves a new one, so keep both until you are sure.
  • Muting to hide something said, then posting the original. Share the muted copy only.

More questions

Can I mute only part of a video?

Not yet: the whole sound track is removed. Trim the part you want first if only that part matters.

Will the muted video play on my phone?

Yes. Nothing about the picture changes, so whatever played the original plays the muted copy.

Can I replace the sound with music?

Not here. Mute the video, then add music in the app where you post it, which also keeps you within its music licence.

Privacy

Nothing you add is uploaded. The page reads your video from your device, copies its picture into a new file in this tab and saves it to your downloads. Nothing is remembered: the video and its name are not kept. There is no account.

How it works

  1. Choose a video: MP4, MOV, WebM or MKV.
  2. The page says whether the picture can be copied untouched (nearly always) or has to be re-encoded.
  3. Press Remove the sound. The new file has the same picture and no sound track at all.

What people use it for

  • Background music you cannot use — Remove a song before posting a clip, then add your own sound in the app.
  • Private conversations — Share what a video shows without what was said behind the camera.
  • Silent loops — Make a looping clip for a website or a presentation that should never make a sound.
  • Wind and traffic noise — Drop a soundtrack that is only noise.

Tips

  • The picture is copied, not re-encoded, so the muted file is the original’s size less its sound.
  • MOV and MKV videos come out as MP4; WebM stays WebM.
  • To keep the sound but lose the picture, use Extract Audio.

Limits and browser support

Files up to 2 GB; phones may run short of memory before that.

A picture format an MP4 cannot hold as it is (VP8 inside an MKV, for example) is re-encoded, and the page says so first.

Frequently asked questions

Does muting change the picture quality?

No. The stored picture frames are copied into the new file byte for byte; only the sound track is left out.

Is the sound turned down or removed?

Removed. The new file has no sound track, so it cannot be turned back up.

How long does it take?

A few seconds for most videos, because nothing is decoded or encoded: the page only copies the picture’s data into a new file.

Which format do I get?

An MP4 for MP4, MOV and MKV videos; a WebM for WebM videos.

Can I keep the sound as a separate file?

Yes: Extract Audio saves the sound of a video as M4A, Ogg or WAV, or as an MP3 when that is how the video stores it.

Is my video uploaded?

No. The page reads the file from your device and builds the muted copy in this tab.
