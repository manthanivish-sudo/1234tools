Frequently asked questions

Do these business tools send my figures or spreadsheets anywhere?

No. Every tool on this page runs in your browser. A spreadsheet you open is read on your device and the results are written there too; close the tab and nothing remains with us.

Are the UK tax and VAT figures current?

Each tool says on its own page which tax year and rules it uses, and the statutory tools carry the date the guidance was checked. They compute the figures; they do not file anything, and HMRC’s guidance is the authority.

Can I use these for Indian payroll and GST as well?

Yes. The payroll run, the CTC structure, the GSTR-2B reconciler and the e-invoice JSON generator follow Indian rules, and the India section has the GST, income tax and TDS calculators.
