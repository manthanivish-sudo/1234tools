How it works

  1. Send the link. The form below asks for your name, where the thing is posted, the link, and the tool you used. No account.
  2. A person reads it. Nothing goes up on its own. If it opens, it is public, and it was made with a tool from this site, it goes on the list.
  3. Your name goes up with it. A card on this page and on the tool’s own page, each with a link to your post.

What we publish

Your name, your handle if you gave one, the platform, the link, the tool, and your line about what you made. What you do, if you picked it, is used to list your entry with others in the same line of work. Nothing else. Your email, if you leave one, is used to tell you when the entry is live and is never shown. Nothing from the platform is copied or embedded: the page is text and a link, so opening it contacts nobody but us.

How to be removed

Ask on the contact page with the link to your entry and it comes down at the next update of the site. You do not need to give a reason. The privacy policy says what is kept and for how long.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything, or need an account?

No. Sending something in is free and needs no account, and so is being listed. Nothing is sold here: the page exists so the people who make good things with these tools get the credit.

Why is there no picture or preview of what I made?

Because showing one would mean loading it from Instagram, YouTube or wherever it lives, and every page on this site promises not to contact anybody else when it opens. So each entry is your name, a line about what you made and a link to it. The views, the likes and the comments all stay on your post, where they count.

How long does it take to appear?

Every entry is read by a person before it goes up, usually within a week. If you left an email you will hear when it is live; it then shows here and on the page of the tool you used.

What gets turned down?

Links that do not open or are not public, things not made with a tool from this site, anything sent on somebody else’s behalf without their say-so, and anything we would not be happy to put next to our own name. A refusal is not a judgement of the work; you are welcome to send something else.

How do I take my entry down?

Ask on the contact page with the link to your entry, and it comes off at the next update of the site — no reason needed. An approved entry stays until you ask; one that was never approved is deleted ninety days after it came in.
