                Testimonials

                Named client quotes, each used only with the client's written permission.

                How to use this page
                1. Ask: fill in Request email, copy it (or the WhatsApp text) and send it yourself.
                2. Record the reply: paste their exact words and the permission details, then Save.
                3. Approve and publish it, then use Export at the bottom for the site.
                Named quotes only, each with the client's written permission. Ask every client the same way, with no suggested wording and nothing in return; paste their words exactly as they sent them. A quote can be approved, published or exported only once you record that they said yes, how, on what date, where you keep their reply, and how to contact them. A withdrawn quote leaves every export at once and lists each page or post it is still on. The desk sends nothing: you send the request yourself. Data:
