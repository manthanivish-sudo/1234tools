How to convert kg to pounds

To convert kilograms to pounds, multiply by 2.20462, or divide by 0.45359237 — the exact number of kilograms in a pound: 70 kg is 154.32 lb. For a quick estimate, double the kilograms and add 10%: 70 × 2 = 140, plus 14, is 154.

7 min read · 5 steps · 5 ways it goes wrong

What you need before you start

  • The weight in kilograms, or in grams divided by 1,000.
  • For UK body weight, whether the answer is wanted in pounds or in stones and pounds.

1. Multiply by 2.20462

pounds = kilograms × 2.20462

pounds = kilograms ÷ 0.45359237 (exact)

The pound is defined in kilograms, so the exact way is to divide by 0.45359237. Multiplying by 2.20462 gives the same answer to five decimal places, which is far more than any bathroom scale can show. 70 kg is 154.3236 lb, which for most purposes is 154.3 lb.

The figures here, and when they were last checked

The international avoirdupois pound — the pound used for body weight, food and luggage — is exactly 0.453 592 37 kilogram. NIST notes that after the 1959 agreement the US and British pounds were the same, and gives that definition; UK law states the same figure and defines the stone as 14 pounds and the ounce as 1/16 pound. The kilogram itself has been defined since the 2018 revision of the SI by fixing the numerical value of the Planck constant, so the pound is tied to a constant of nature through it.

Checked against the sources below on 4 October 2026. Rates, thresholds and deadlines move — at a Budget, at a notification, sometimes between one. Anything you are going to act on, check at the source.

  • legislation.gov.uk — Weights and Measures Act 1985, Schedule 1 — the pound as 0.453 592 37 kilogram, the stone and the ounce
  • NIST Handbook 44 (2026), Appendix B — Units and Systems of Measurement — the avoirdupois pound defined in terms of the kilogram, and the 1959 agreement
  • BIPM — SI base unit: the kilogram — the kilogram defined by the Planck constant

2. Use the quick method when an estimate will do

Double the kilograms, then add a tenth of that. For a 23 kg suitcase: 23 × 2 = 46, plus 4.6, is 50.6 lb. The exact answer is 50.71 lb, so the shortcut is about 0.2% low — close enough for a suitcase, a parcel or a conversation.

It works because 2 × 1.1 = 2.2, which is just under the real factor of 2.20462.

3. Turn pounds into stones and pounds

In the UK, body weight is often given in stones and pounds. A stone is 14 pounds. Convert to pounds, divide by 14 for the whole stones, and the remainder is the pounds. 82 kg is 180.78 lb; 14 goes into 180 twelve times, which is 168 lb, leaving 12.78 lb. So 82 kg is 12 st 12.8 lb.

70 kg is 154.32 lb, which is 11 stone exactly (154 lb) and 0.32 lb more: 11 st 0.3 lb.

When a limit is set in one unit, convert the limit and compare in that unit. A 23 kg bag weighs 50.71 lb, so it is over a 50 lb limit; 50 lb is 22.68 kg. The two figures are often printed side by side as if they were the same, and they are not quite.

5. Going the other way: pounds to kilograms

kilograms = pounds × 0.45359237

Multiply by the exact definition: 154 lb is 69.85 kg, and 50 lb is 22.68 kg. The rough version is to halve the pounds and take off a tenth: 154 ÷ 2 = 77, less 7.7, is 69.3 — less than 1% out.

Where this usually goes wrong

5 things that actually happen, rather than a note asking you to be careful.

  • Dividing by 2.2 when you meant to multiply. 70 ÷ 2.2 is 31.8, which is 70 pounds in kilograms. A pound is lighter than a kilogram, so going from kilograms to pounds the number must get bigger.
  • Reading 11.5 stone as 11 stone 5 pounds. A stone has 14 pounds, not 10, so 11.5 stone is 11 st 7 lb. The decimal part has to be multiplied by 14 before it means pounds.
  • Treating 23 kg and 50 lb as the same limit. 23 kg is 50.71 lb. A bag weighed at exactly 23 kg is over a 50 lb limit, and one at 22.7 kg is under it. Convert the limit, not the bag, and compare in the limit’s own unit.
  • Rounding the factor to 2. Doubling alone undershoots by about 9%: 100 kg becomes 200 lb instead of 220.46. Add the extra tenth, or use the exact factor.
  • Using the wrong kind of pound or ounce. Gold and silver are priced by the troy ounce, which is heavier than the ordinary ounce; the UK abolished the troy pound in 1879. The converter’s pound and ounce are the avoirdupois units used for everything else.

How long this should take

Seconds. The only judgement is how precise the answer needs to be — a suitcase, a recipe and a dose are different jobs.

Frequently asked questions

How many pounds are in a kilogram?

About 2.20462. More precisely 2.2046226218, the result of dividing 1 by 0.45359237.

How many kilograms are in a pound?

Exactly 0.45359237. It is the definition of the pound, so it has no further decimal places.

What is 70 kg in stones and pounds?

11 st 0.3 lb. 70 kg is 154.32 lb, and 11 stone is 154 lb.

Is a pound the same in the UK and the US?

Yes. Both use the international avoirdupois pound of exactly 0.45359237 kg.

Why do converters give 154.3 or 154.32 for 70 kg?

Only because of rounding. The exact value is 154.32358…; how many decimals are shown is a choice, not a different answer.
