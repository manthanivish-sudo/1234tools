Passport Photo 35 × 45 mm

35 × 45 mm is the passport photo size of the UK, the Schengen area, Australia and most of the world. Frame your picture to it below.

Set for this page: Other: ICAO standard 35×45 mm, 35 × 45 mm, with a 6 × 4 in print sheet. Every other setting of the Passport & ID Photo Maker is here too.

The 35 × 45 mm standard

The size comes from ICAO Document 9303, the international guide for machine-readable passports, which asks for the head to fill 70 to 80 per cent of the photo’s height. At 300 DPI the photo is 413 pixels wide and 531 high.

Countries agree on the frame but not on every detail: head height, background shade and whether glasses are allowed differ. If you are applying to one country, use its page below, which follows that issuer’s own figures.

DocumentOther: ICAO standard 35×45 mm
Print size35 × 45 mm (1.38 × 1.77 in)
Pixels at 300 DPI413 × 531
Head size32 to 36 mm from chin to crown
Backgroundplain and light
Sourcewww.icao.int

A real example

A head-and-shoulders photo of 1200 × 1296 pixels, framed where the tool first placed it, became a 35 × 45 mm photo of 413 × 531, a JPEG of 74.8 KB, and a 6 × 4 in print sheet of 1800 × 1200, 632.0 KB.

Measured with the tool on this page in Chrome on 7 October 2026. Your own files will give their own figures.

How to make a 35 × 45 mm photo

  1. Choose a front-facing photo against a plain, light wall.
  2. Press “Frame my face automatically”, or drag and zoom until the chin sits on the gold line.
  3. Check the head between the dashed lines.
  4. Download the photo and the 6 × 4 in sheet for a photo shop print.

Privacy

Your images never leave your device. Files are decoded by your own browser and re-encoded by encoders that run inside this page, served from this site, so nothing is uploaded, queued or logged.

Frequently asked questions

How many pixels is a 35 × 45 mm photo?

At 300 DPI, the usual print resolution, 413 × 531 pixels. Online forms that ask for a 35 × 45 photo usually expect about that, or the same shape at a larger size.

Can I print the photo at home or in a shop?

Yes. The 6 × 4 in sheet is the standard photo print size any shop or kiosk prints, with copies laid out and cutting gaps between them; an A4 sheet is under Output for a home printer.

Is the background replaced?

Only if you ask: choose “Replace with the colour below” and the subject is cut out on your device with a small model, then put on the colour you pick.
