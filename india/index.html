Frequently asked questions

Which financial year do the tax calculators use?

The current one, named on each page, with both regimes side by side where the choice matters. When the Budget changes a slab the page is updated and the date of the check is shown.

Is the GST split right for inter-state supplies?

Yes. Choose inter-state and the tax shows as IGST; within a state it is split equally into CGST and SGST.

Do these replace a chartered accountant?

No. They give you the figure and the working so you can check it. Filing, planning and anything unusual belong with a professional.
